Our Story

Founded on Justice & Integrity

Stackly Law was founded in 2002 by a group of passionate attorneys who believed every client deserves exceptional legal representation — regardless of complexity or circumstance.

Over two decades, we've grown from a boutique firm into a full-service legal powerhouse with a team of 40+ attorneys, paralegals, and support staff. Our success is measured not by billable hours, but by lives changed, rights protected, and justice delivered.

  • Over 5,000 cases successfully resolved across all practice areas
  • Certified specialists in 12 distinct areas of law
  • AV Preeminent® rated by Martindale-Hubbell for ethics and legal ability
  • Named "Top Law Firm" by the State Bar Association 7 consecutive years
  • 24/7 emergency legal support for criminal defense clients
